Simply use our "Find a Hostel" search bar to filter by city and preferences. Once you find a hostel you like, click on "View" to see the details, and then follow the instructions to submit a booking request. Our team or the hostel warden will contact you within 24 hours to confirm availability.

Student Shelter does not charge any brokerage or platform commission from students. However, individual hostels may require a refundable security deposit (usually equal to one month's rent). This information is clearly mentioned on the hostel details page.

It depends on the hostel. Most hostels offer mess facilities with 2 or 3 meals a day included in the rent, while others might charge separately. Amenities like Wi-Fi, electricity, and water are usually included in the monthly rent, but optional air-conditioning charges may apply.
