Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about finding and booking a hostel near your university.

Finding a hostel

How do I search for a hostel?

Go to the Hostels page, choose your university or area, and pick your budget. The list updates to show hostels that match.

Can I filter hostels by price?

Yes. Use the price filter on the Hostels page to see only hostels within your monthly budget.

Are the hostel photos and details accurate?

Hostel owners provide the details. We recommend visiting a hostel before you pay any advance.

Rent and rooms

What is included in the monthly rent?

This depends on the hostel. Each listing shows whether meals, electricity, internet and laundry are included.

Can I share a room with a friend?

Many hostels offer shared rooms for two or more students. Check the room types on each hostel's page.

Using this website

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can browse all hostels without signing up.

How can I contact a hostel owner?

Each hostel page lists the owner's contact details. You can also send us a message from the Contact page.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we will reply as soon as we can.
