Our Story

Why We Created Student Shelter

Relocating to a new city for higher education is an exciting milestone, yet thousands of students and parents spend weeks navigating unreliable listings, hidden fees, and unverified hostel conditions.

Student Shelter was built by students who experienced that struggle firsthand. Our mission is simple: connect university learners with safe, transparent, and comfortable hostels within easy reach of campus—without middlemen or guesswork.
